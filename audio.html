THE QUICK GUIDE

Choose a format for your next step.

MP3

Smaller and shareable

Lossy compression makes MP3 convenient for everyday playback and sharing. Re-encoding can reduce quality.

WAV

Uncompressed audio

Useful for editing and broad workflow compatibility, but WAV output can be much larger than MP3.

INPUT

Browser-decoded sources

Inputs include common audio formats such as MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, and FLAC when your browser can decode them.

Audio files are limited to 100 MB and 30 minutes. Files are decoded and re-encoded locally as WAV or 128 kbps MP3. MP3 encoding loads a small library from a CDN. Supported input codecs depend on your browser.
