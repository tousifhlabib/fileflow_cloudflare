GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

No jargon. Just useful answers.

Are my files private?+

Your files are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to a conversion server. Document converters load their processing libraries from a CDN, so you need an internet connection to use them.

What file types can I convert right now?+

Images: PNG, JPG, and WebP. Audio: browser-supported audio to WAV or MP3. Video: supported clips to MP4 up to 1080p, or extract audio as MP3 or WAV. Documents: selected PDF, DOCX, XLSX, XLS, CSV, and TXT conversions. Options depend on the source file and browser.

Do I need to install anything?+

No account or installation is needed. Your browser handles audio, video, and image processing; document conversion libraries load on demand from a CDN.
